# Span 0.5.0 — Your Summit list

Build a searchable desktop attendee list. Add your LinkedIn export to highlight people you might already know.

## New in this version

- One list, with search, possible-connections-first or A–Z sorting, and Everyone / Possible connections / Confirmed by you filters.
- Expand any attendee to compare event details with possible identities in your export. No match is automatically called first degree.
- Open a candidate’s profile separately from searching LinkedIn for the attendee.
- Confirm or dismiss individual pairings, undo decisions, and keep your place. Dismissing a suggestion never removes an attendee or labels them not connected.
- Existing automatic matches are recomputed as suggestions. Human decisions and saved inputs are retained; guessed legacy profile links are cleared.
- CSV exports separate confirmed profile URLs from possible profile URLs. The list can be exported without a LinkedIn archive.

## Requirements and limits

Apple-silicon Mac, macOS 13 or later, and access to Lenny & Friends. A LinkedIn connections export is optional for browsing and needed for suggestions. Profile-detail pages are not crawled in this version; see [the v2 roadmap](../roadmap.md).

The collector is unchanged from the development build that collected all 1,355 attendees from the bottom of the event list. This revision requires its own installer pilot before publication. Name/company similarity is not proof of identity. Confirmed by you reflects your decision, not independent verification by Span.
