# Span 0.5.1 — Summit attendees you may know

A simpler way to spot familiar faces at the summit. One useful list, with possible LinkedIn connections visible immediately.

## What changed

- One heading and an honest comparison summary using your actual attendee and export counts.
- Possible matches appear as a quiet green subline beneath the attendee’s event details. No expansion needed to see who the suggestion is.
- Open a row to review. “Search LinkedIn for the attendee” stays beside the event details; “Open your connection’s profile” stays beside the person from your export. Neither is styled as a warning.
- Same person, Different person, and row-local Undo keep the list in place. Your confirmed and dismissed suggestions remain available to revisit.
- Other attendees continue below the suggestions. Search is tucked behind a small Search control (or Command-F); export is at the bottom.
- Removed the filter bar, sort picker, global feedback strip, Refresh order action, and promotional card from the list view. The header and setup experience are unchanged.

## Requirements and limits

Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 13+, and access to the Lenny & Friends event app. Add your LinkedIn connections export for suggestions, or browse without one. Span processes those inputs locally; clicking a LinkedIn link opens LinkedIn in your browser.

Suggestions are not identity verification. Only your explicit choice marks an attendee as confirmed. No suggestion does not mean you are not connected. This version reads attendee list cards, not each profile-detail page. The collector and identity-matching rules are unchanged from 0.5.0; saved inputs and decisions are retained.

## Release verification

The release workflow runs automated tests, signs the app and disk image, submits the disk image to Apple, staples the accepted notarization ticket, and checks its signature. This release remains a draft until the exact installer passes installation testing. Do not mistake signing/notarization for an independent fresh-Mac usability pilot.
